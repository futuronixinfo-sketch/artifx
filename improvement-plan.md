# Artifx.dev Improvement Plan

## Objective

Transform Artifx from a developer-focused portfolio into a high-converting agency website that builds trust with startup founders, business owners, and enterprise clients.

---

# Priority 1: Increase Trust Signals

## Problem

The website looks premium but lacks strong credibility indicators.

Visitors should quickly understand:

* Who is behind the company
* Why they should trust the company
* What results the company has delivered

## Solution

### Add Founder Section

Include:

* Founder photo
* Founder name
* Short introduction
* LinkedIn profile link
* Years of experience
* Technologies and expertise

Example:

"Built digital products for startups and businesses using React, Next.js, Spring Boot, AI, and cloud technologies."

---

### Add Team Section

Display:

* Team members
* Roles
* Expertise

This makes the company feel real and trustworthy.

---

### Add Client Logos

Create a section:

Trusted By Businesses Across Industries

Display:

* Client logos
* Partner logos
* Startup logos

Even if projects are small, logos create immediate trust.

---

### Add Testimonials

Include:

* Client name
* Company
* Role
* Project delivered
* Review

Optional:

Video testimonials

---

# Priority 2: Improve Hero Messaging

## Current Issue

The hero feels technical and developer-centric.

Business owners may not immediately understand the value.

## Goal

Communicate business outcomes.

### Recommended Headline

Build Websites, Apps & AI Systems That Grow Your Business

### Supporting Text

We help startups and businesses launch scalable digital products, automate operations, and increase revenue through technology.

### Primary CTA

Book a Discovery Call

### Secondary CTA

View Case Studies

---

# Priority 3: Make Services Easier to Understand

## Current Issue

Some terminology feels too technical.

Examples:

* SYSTEM_FIRST_ARCHITECTURE
* NODE_01
* AI_NATIVE
* OPERATIONAL_CORE

These sound impressive but may confuse decision makers.

## Replace With

### Website Development

Modern websites built for performance and conversions.

### Web Applications

Custom business software and SaaS platforms.

### Mobile Applications

iOS and Android solutions.

### AI Automation

Reduce manual work through AI-powered workflows.

### Product Design

User experiences that convert visitors into customers.

---

# Priority 4: Strengthen Case Studies

## Current Issue

Projects are showcased but business outcomes are not emphasized enough.

## Required Structure

### Challenge

What problem did the client face?

### Solution

What was built?

### Result

Show measurable outcomes.

Examples:

* Increased leads by 220%
* Reduced manual work by 70%
* Improved conversion rate by 35%
* Reduced operational costs by 40%

Business results sell more effectively than technical achievements.
