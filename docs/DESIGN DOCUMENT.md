# Artifx – Design System Document

## 1. Brand Personality
- Minimal
- Premium
- Confident
- Intelligent
- System-driven

---

## 2. Design Philosophy
> “Less design, more clarity.”

Focus on:
- Readability
- Structure
- Spacing
- System flow visuals

---

## 3. Color System

Primary:
- Black #000000
- White #FFFFFF

Accent:
- Soft gradient (AI feel)
- Neutral gray shades

Avoid:
- Too many colors
- Bright childish tones

---

## 4. Typography

Headings:
- Bold
- Large
- Confident

Body:
- Clean
- Simple
- Business-readable

---

## 5. Layout Rules
- Max-width: 1200px
- Grid-based sections
- Section spacing: large
- Focus on storytelling

---

## 6. Card Design
- Black background cards
- Subtle glass blur
- Rounded-xl or 2xl
- Soft border

---

## 7. Hero Section Design

Desktop:
- Left: Message
- Right: System illustration / abstract visual

Mobile:
- Center aligned
- Strong headline
- One CTA only

---

## 8. Visual Elements
- Flow diagrams
- System blocks
- Automation icons
- AI-style gradients

---

## 9. UX Principles
- One primary CTA per section
- Simple language
- No clutter
- Scroll-based storytelling

---

## 10. Future Brand Assets
- Case study templates
- SaaS landing templates
- Pitch deck theme
