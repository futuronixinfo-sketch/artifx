# Artifx – Agency Website PRD

## 1. Product Overview
Artifx is a modern digital systems and AI solutions agency website.
The goal is to position Artifx not as a “website development agency”,
but as a **Business Systems & AI Automation Company**.

The website will:
- Generate high-quality leads
- Build long-term brand credibility
- Showcase systems-based solutions
- Support future SaaS product launches

---

## 2. Target Audience
- Startup founders
- Service-based businesses
- Agencies
- SMEs looking to automate operations
- Founders wanting scalable tech systems

---

## 3. Core Goals
- Strong first impression (premium + trustworthy)
- Clear differentiation from freelancers
- Explain complex systems in simple language
- Convert visitors into booked calls
- Prepare foundation for SaaS ecosystem

---

## 4. Key Value Proposition
> “Artifx builds intelligent digital systems that help businesses scale faster using AI and modern technology.”

---
